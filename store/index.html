Quick answers

Questions

Who prints and ships the orders?

Spreadshirt, a print-on-demand company. They print each item when it is ordered and ship it from the United States. Shipping costs and times are listed in the shop.

Can I return something?

Yes, through Spreadshirt. Their returns policy is linked at the bottom of the shop, and their service address is there too.

Does buying something support the mods and apps?

Yes. Each sale pays a small design commission, which goes toward the same pot as the ads and affiliate links on this site: keeping the mods, the apps and the hosting free.
