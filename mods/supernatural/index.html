Supernatural

Vampires, werewolves, hybrids, witches, demons and the hunters who go after them, in the base game. Every creature is a character with a name and a court, every feeding leaves a trail, and every hunt is aimed at somebody who did something. Play the hunter, or rule as something far older.

Core features

What the mod adds

The blood

Vampires feed, and it costs the county. A clean feed leaves it poorer and harder to hold, a drained county is easier to feed in next time, and a bite that takes will wake a man hungry. Refuse the blood twice and it kills him. Every vampire has a maker and every maker has progeny, and the ranks run from Fledgling to Elder.

The moon, and the hybrid

A werewolf's wound festers for one to three years before it turns. Werewolves rank from Fledgling to Primal, and a known hybrid frightens a court more than a known vampire does. Six lifestyles and ten perk trees cover vampires, werewolves, hunters, hybrids, magic and demons, and the demon tree alone is sixteen perks.

The hunt

Since 2.35 the monsters leave trails. A feeding or a kill marks the county, the lord notices, and a real hunter runs a Supernatural Hunt there: a Monster Hunt or a Witch Hunt, three weeks of tracking to work out what it is and who it is, with the creature getting its own side of every beat. Hunters train apprentices, field their own regiment, and keep a Trailsman at court who reads the countryside.

Regiments and artifacts

Seven supernatural regiments, vampires and werewolves among them, plus cultists, skeletons, demonic knights and golems. Artifacts to find or make, the Ring of Everlasting, the Magic Catalyst and the Black Grimoire among them.

The craft

Witches brew. The Witch's Brew, the Love Potion, the Witch's Mutagen and the Elixir of Life are decisions for a witch with an herbalist or the right perk, and compulsion lets a vampire bend anyone whose blood is not already something else.

The look of it

A red main menu and eighteen loading screens, so the game looks like the mod from the moment it opens.

Customize your journey

Your arcane, your rules

Twenty-one game rules decide how much of this touches your campaign: how traits are inherited (eight settings), how demons pass theirs down, how common the creatures are, and how hard the hunters come. Hunt it, rule with it, or leave it at the edges of the map.

Two ways in

Getting the mod

Free either way. The Workshop is the easy one; the manual install is for anyone whose copy of CK3 didn't come from Steam.

On Steam

One click, it keeps itself current, and the launcher sorts it out for you. If you have the choice, take this one.

Subscribe on the Workshop

GOG, Epic, Game Pass

The mod is plain files, so it runs on any copy of the game. Download it from the repository and drop it in by hand. It takes about ten minutes, and the guide walks every step.

Download without Steam

Playing it alongside The Long Night & Azor Ahai? Both mods rewrite some of the same files, so you'll want the compatibility patch — loaded last.
