Before you play: one rule

Disable every other Long Night mod

AGOT: The Long Night and The Long Night+ must be unchecked in your playset — not sorted below this one, disabled.

This mod grew out of both and ships files at the same paths. Either of them loaded anywhere in your list overrides this mod with older code and brings back the see-through dragon bug. You can unsubscribe from them entirely — this mod replaces both.

What you'll play

The dead get back up

Not a doom counter and a stack of skeletons. An invasion that has characters, politics and a body count you recognise.

Wights are characters, not troops

Every man you lose can rise wearing his own face — wights with their own portraits, their own titles, their own rot, ruling their own realms under the Night King.

They raise what they kill

Bring a dragon down over the Haunted Forest and it comes back. Same name, same face. The wrong colour, and the wrong rider.

The Wall comes down on camera

A scripted collapse at Castle Black, not a pop-up notification. And it's one of five ways the dark can arrive — the breach, the last ranging that came back wrong, the free folk exodus, or the quiet one, where it was already inside your hall wearing a face you knew.

Take the black, and mean it

Lay down your lands, hand the realm to your heir, and serve. Hold one of the Wall's nineteen castles as a sworn brother — or ride out with one of eight ranging companies, landless and oathbound, each with a real camp on the map from day one. Captains compete for First Ranger.

Decisions only the Lord Commander gets

Call a Great Ranging north for word, and pay two hundred brothers for it. Or send a wandering crow gate to gate down the length of Westeros, asking every great lord the old question: how full are your dungeons, and what were you planning to do with the men inside them?

Southern lords have a stake

Send Men and Grain to the Watch — a cart, a season's stores, or your whole vault. The Watch keeps a list, and it decides what they'll do for you later.

A century of warning

You can watch it coming

The Long Night is a full four-phase struggle. The world pushes the dark closer on its own — dead dragons, lost rangings, a hollowed-out Watch, an Other seen and believed.

THE LONG SUMMER

Decades of quiet, and everything you fail to do with them. There is a visible counter. Most players ignore it exactly once.

WINTER IS COMING

The rangings stop coming back. A warning of unknown length — and it can get shorter.

THE LONG NIGHT

The invasion proper. What you did with the warning — dragonglass in the cellars, men and grain sent north, a realm that armed instead of arguing — is the whole difference between surviving this and being surprised by it.

THE DAWN

What is left, and what it cost.

A quiet century still ends on schedule. A bad one ends early.

The prophecy

Azor Ahai is a mechanic, not a title

Not a ruler-designer trait you tick at the start. Something the world works out about you, and sometimes gets wrong.

Eight deeds mark you

  • Dragon dreams, and greensight
  • Born in salt and smoke, or beneath a bleeding star
  • Walking among the dead and surviving it
  • Carrying spellforged steel
  • Waking dragons from stone
  • Dying, and not staying dead

Two of them are checked continuously — lose the sword or the dragon and the claim goes with it.

And the realm decides what it means

  • Deeds need validating by a red priest, a witch or a greenseer — an unwitnessed miracle counts for nothing
  • Three deeds make you Favored of R'hllor; six make you Chosen
  • Lightbringer can be forged, at the price every version of the tale agrees on
  • Kill the Night King and you are Azor Ahai Reborn, named or not
  • The AI plays all of it — rival claimants meet, and sometimes ally

Whether the one they named was ever the prince is decided behind the curtain, and the world only finds out at the end. There are four ways that can land.

After

The North is scoured

The Question of the North

Restore the Night's Watch, or open the Gift to the free folk. One crown, one choice, and it sticks for the rest of the game.

The land refills

Wildling clans migrate into the empty North year by year. Landless survivors walk the Kingsroad home to rebuild their ancestral seats.

Sell the ashes

Sell colonisation rights to the Free Cities — then watch your northern vassals remember that you did.

Your apocalypse, your rules

Make it happen when you want it

Game rules

  • How likely — certain, through to never
  • When — immediately, in 50 / 100 / 150 years, historical, random, or only when you say so
  • How hard — weak through to insane
  • Whether the Night King gets his dragon
  • Whether the Iron Throne reforms afterward

And whether it happens again

Turn the wheel on: the Wall is raised anew, the veterans of the first dawn are known by name, and the second night comes heavier than the first.

Setup

Load order

  1. A Game of Thrones — required
  2. AGOT submods — AGOT+, Valyrian Steel, Submod Core, Nobility, model packs…
  3. AGOT: The Long Night & Azor Ahai (this mod)
  4. Optional: Supernatural
  5. If you run Supernatural: AGOT: Supernatural & The Long Night Compatibility Patch — always last

One note for dragon-mod users

Dead dragons' looks are baked into your save against the exact list of portrait genes your mods add. Adding or removing any gene-adding mod mid-save — this one included — can scramble dragons that are already dead. Living ones are safe. Pick your dragon mods at campaign start and keep them.

Full disclosure

What this overrides from AGOT

Kept deliberately short, because you deserve to know what a submod touches. Each is AGOT's own file with a named, documented change, re-checked against their current version every release.

Three files

  • Scripted rules — so a landless brother of the Watch can move his ranging camp the way the First Ranger does, and so a sworn brother can't end up regent of a southern kingdom
  • Domicile types — so a ranging captain can hold a camp at all. AGOT built the ranger camp for exactly one title
  • The Night's Watch title-gain handler — so appointing a ranging captain doesn't force a government change on him

Everything else

Sits alongside AGOT rather than on top of it. The mod is safe to add and to update mid-save — though game rules are baked in when a save is created, so an existing campaign keeps whatever rules it started with.

Built on the work of others. This is a fork standing on Freeholder's original Long Night, Tehaen's 1.19 update, and Wepo94's The Long Night+.

For the ones who read this far

There is a door in this mod

It has no handle on the outside. It opens for one kind of lord, it is checked exactly once — at the moment your character first draws breath — and nobody has ever stumbled into it by accident.

The riddle of the household ledger

Three decisions sit behind it. They do not appear greyed out with their requirements listed; if you are not the one it is waiting for, they do not exist. That is deliberate.

Everything you need is below. Nothing below is a lie.

Found your own house — and answer to no one above you.
Take the sin the septons put in the flesh,
and the virtue they put in the work. Both.
They sit oddly together. That is rather the point.
Then set your sword-arm at three fives —
not one more, and not one less.

Do all of it in the ruler designer, before the first snow. The door is checked once and never again, so a build that drifts into shape later will find nothing waiting.

What's behind it: a household to put in order, a house to write down, and a ship that came back from somewhere ships do not come back from. That last one is not small.

No console, no save editing, no downloads. Just a character sheet built a particular way.

Common questions

Before you subscribe

Do I really have to disable the other Long Night mods?

Yes, and it's the single most common cause of something being broken. This mod is a fork of both AGOT: The Long Night and The Long Night+, and it ships files at the same paths. Load order won't save you — either of them enabled anywhere in your playset overrides this mod with older code. Disabled, not sorted. You can unsubscribe from them entirely.

Do I need to start a new game?

No. It's safe to add and to update mid-save. The one caveat is that CK3 bakes game rules in when a save is created, so an existing campaign keeps whatever rules it started with — if you want to change the invasion timing or difficulty, that needs a fresh save.

Does it work with Supernatural?

Yes, with the compatibility patch. Load this mod, then Supernatural, then AGOT: Supernatural & The Long Night Compatibility Patch last. Supernatural has its own page here if you haven't seen it.

What if I use other dragon mods?

Pick them at campaign start and keep them. Dead dragons' appearances are stored against the exact set of portrait genes loaded at the time, so adding or removing any gene-adding mod mid-save can scramble dragons that have already died. Living dragons are unaffected.

What version of CK3 is it for?

CK3 1.19.0.6, built against the current release of A Game of Thrones.

Is it translated?

English and Russian, both complete.

Where do I report a bug or suggest something?

The Long Night Discord is the fastest route — bug reports, ideas and questions all land there, and player input has driven a lot of what's in the mod. The Steam Workshop comments work too.
