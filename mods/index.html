The compatibility patch

Want vampires and the army of the dead in the same campaign? Both mods rewrite some of the same files, and whichever loads second silently erases the other's version. This fixes that.

AGOT: Supernatural & The Long Night Compatibility Patch

needs all three mods

It does nothing on its own — and it has to load last.

Twenty-two entries that both mods define. Each one is a three-way merge against the base game's own version, so neither mod's changes are guessed at.

  • Marriage acceptance — AGOT's full 1,000-line house and legitimacy logic, plus Supernatural's fertility exemptions. Without the patch one of the two is simply gone; this entry alone was behind multiplayer desyncs in testing.
  • Births — AGOT's entire birth pipeline (house looks, Valyrian genetics, dragon dreams) and Supernatural's, in one file. Both ship it at the same path, so the later one was deleting the other wholesale.
  • The Originals — Supernatural's founding vampire family is written for the vanilla map, with a Norse culture and faith that don't exist in Westeros. Under the patch they become First Men of the North: northman culture, Old Gods faith.
  • Plus disinheritance, the witch ritual, secrets, schemes, abductions, artifact demands, traits, imprisonment and resurrection rules.

Load order — drag the patch to the very bottom

  1. A Game of Thrones
  2. AGOT submods — AGOT+, Valyrian Steel, Submod Core, Nobility…
  3. AGOT: The Long Night & Azor Ahai
  4. Supernatural
  5. The Compatibility Patch — this one, last

Every fix works by defining the entry the two mods fight over. Anything loaded after it wins instead, and the patch does nothing at all.
