The plan

What we're building

A guild for leveling, grouping up and hanging out on the North American (NA) servers, with players from every time zone welcome. The end game is still open; we'll pick it together once we see what Forever becomes.

Some of us ran together on The Burning Crusade Anniversary. We left those names there. Forever is where we're staying.

The Discord is where the guild lives between sessions: who's playing what, who needs a group, and what we're running next.

House rules

Everything within reason

That's our unofficial motto, and it covers most of the rules.

  • Everyone's welcome. Who you are is your business, and nobody here gives anyone grief for it.
  • Politics and sexuality stay out of guild chat. That's not a judgment of anyone. Neither has anything to do with WoW, and they're the fastest way to split a guild.
  • You get one friendly warning. After that, you'll need to find another guild.
  • A little chaos is fine. Drama isn't. We're building for the people who stay.
The name

Why the Grey Company

It's Tolkien's. In The Lord of the Rings, the Grey Company are the Rangers of the North who ride south to join Aragorn and follow him through the Paths of the Dead. The Star Wars fans in the Discord heard Grey Jedi instead. Both work.

Before you join

Quick answers

Is it a raiding guild?

Not to start. We're leveling and social. Once people hit 60 and the new raids open, we'll decide together how far to take it.

Which region and time zone?

NA, the North American servers. I'm on US Eastern time, but if you play on NA you're welcome from any time zone.

Can I join if I play Horde?

The guild is Alliance. On the Normal ruleset you can make characters on both factions, so a Horde alt is fine; you just can't group with us on it.

Do I need to buy anything extra?

No. Forever is included with a WoW subscription or Game Time. The upgrades are optional; one only matters if you want to reserve your name early, 27 October to 3 November.

What about Hardcore?

We're on Normal. Hardcore is its own ruleset, which Blizzard said would open after the others. Characters can't move between rulesets, except that a Hardcore character who dies can transfer to another one.

The game

WoW Forever, the short version

What Blizzard has announced, checked against its own posts on 8 October 2026. Blizzard can change details, and its posts linked below are the final word. New to Forever? Read the beginner's guide.

  • Launch: 4 November 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST (6:00 p.m. Eastern).
  • What it is: a new, permanent version of WoW alongside the modern game and Classic. It's set before Molten Core and starts on the original continents with a level cap of 60.
  • Cost: included with a WoW subscription or Game Time. Upgrade packs are optional.
  • Name reservation: 27 October to 3 November 2026 (PST), with any upgrade. Up to three characters, first come, first served.
  • No realm list: you pick a ruleset instead: Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, or Hardcore (Blizzard said Hardcore would open after the others). Switching means a new character. We're on Normal.
  • Two names: every character has a first and a second name, and the full name has to be unique in your region.
  • Factions: Horde and Alliance still can't group together.

Sources, all Blizzard: launch, cost and name reservation, rulesets and factions, character names, and what Forever is. The game's own page is worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/forever. World of Warcraft is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment; this is a player guild, not an official Blizzard page.

Who's behind it

My last stop in Azeroth

WoW is where my name comes from. Dlonem is what the game's random name generator gave me for a death knight in sixth grade, give or take a few letters I changed. Before that I was Noaha, a warlock who joined Icecrown Citadel raids he had no business being in. The full story is on the Legacy page.

I'm not going back to the modern game, and the only thing that would pull me back to Anniversary is Wrath. Blizzard calls Forever a permanent home it plans to keep building, starting on the original continents at level 60. If it lives up to that, it's the last version of WoW I need, and The Grey Company is built to last as long as it does.

I've run Discord servers since 2016, and the rooms that last are the ones where people can relax. My first website existed to solve the can't-paste-an-invite problem for The Game Center. dlonem.com/grey is the same fix, years later.
