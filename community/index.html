Four servers, one host

I've been building Discord communities since Discord was the new kid. Pick the room that fits.

Est. May 2016 — the original

The Game Center

Started in May 2016 as a Skype replacement for my friend group — I'd found Discord before most people had heard of it and made us a server with a plain controller icon. It was never meant to be a "community project"; it just kept growing into one. Holiday merch giveaways, game nights, years of running jokes, eventually its own website. The icon changed with every season; the friendships didn't.
