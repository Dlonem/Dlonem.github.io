If you only read one thing

The short answer

If you are a casual player, or new to WoW, play a Hunter. It is the easiest class to understand.

THOMAS, DLONEM

A Hunter's pet holds the enemy's attention while you attack from range, so most fights are forgiving. Warrior and Paladin are the other two I would point a new player at. Pick whichever race you like the look of, and play the faction your friends are on.

Hunter

Damage

A pet holds the enemy while you attack from range, which makes it the easiest class to understand.

My pick for a casual or brand-new player. Plan for ammunition and pet taming: years ago, they were the two things that caught me out.

Fights: Ranged (Survival fights up close) · Heaviest armor: Mail
Specs: Beast Mastery, Marksmanship, Survival

  • Pets gain stats from your gear, and every pet family can learn Bite or Claw.
  • Foxes are a new pet family.
  • Traps work in combat now, and Aimed Shot is a normal ability at level 20.
  • No stuns or interrupts, and ranged attacks do not work within 8 yards.

Alliance: Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Skyborne (High Order)
Horde: Orc, Tauren, Troll, Skyborne (Windshaper)

Warrior

TankDamage

Blizzard says a Warrior fits any group as a tank or a damage dealer.

One of the two I would point a new player at, after the Hunter.

Fights: Melee · Heaviest armor: Plate
Specs: Arms, Fury, Protection

  • Victory Rush arrives at level 20: kill something and you can hit back for a heal.
  • Tactical Mastery is baseline at 14, so you keep 10 Rage when you switch stances.
  • Critical hits give 75% more Rage.
  • Blizzard's warning: few ways to recover from heavy damage.

Alliance: Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, Skyborne (High Order)
Horde: Orc, Undead, Tauren, Troll, Skyborne (Windshaper)

Paladin

TankHealerDamage

Heavy armor and healing of your own, and it can tank, heal or deal damage.

The other class I would point a new player at.

Fights: Melee · Heaviest armor: Plate
Specs: Holy, Protection, Retribution

  • Undead Paladin is new: in Forever, Horde players can be Paladins.
  • Blessing of Kings is now a normal Paladin spell, not a talent.
  • Human, Dwarf and Undead are the only races that can be one.

Alliance: Human, Dwarf
Horde: Undead

HealerDamage

Shaman

HealerDamage

Melee or spell damage, or healing, in mail with a shield, and totems that help the whole group.

Fights: Melee or ranged · Heaviest armor: Mail
Specs: Elemental, Enhancement, Restoration

  • Dwarf Shaman is new: the Alliance gets Shamans.
  • Totems are on a 1-second global cooldown, and buff totems last 5 minutes.
  • Call of the Elements (level 20) drops all four totems in one cast.
  • Blizzard says Enhancement can tank small groups for a while.

Alliance: Dwarf
Horde: Orc, Tauren, Troll, Skyborne (Windshaper)

Druid

TankHealerDamage

Blizzard says a Druid can fill any role with the right gear and talents.

Fights: Melee or ranged · Heaviest armor: Leather
Specs: Balance, Feral, Restoration

  • All potions and almost all similar items now work in animal forms.
  • Revive is a new out-of-combat resurrection at level 12.

Who can be what

Races and classes

From Blizzard's race and class list. Six pairs are new to Forever: Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Gnome Priest, Orc Mage, Undead Paladin and Troll Warlock. The Skyborne are a new race on both factions: the Alliance's High Order can be Mages, the Horde's Windshapers can be Shamans, and both can be Druids, Hunters, Rogues and Warriors. They start on Zephras Isle and come with the Forever upgrade packs.

Once you have chosen

Talents and trainers

  • You learn spells from your class trainer as you level, and they cost gold. Visit one every couple of levels.
  • Talents start at level 10. Each tree has bigger talents at 11, 16, 21 and 31 points; the 16-point one is new to Forever.
  • To change your talents, visit a class trainer in a major city.
  • Dual specialization arrives at level 40, so you can keep two sets of talents.

Quick answers

Questions

What is the easiest class in WoW Forever?

For a new or casual player, the Hunter. Your pet holds the enemy while you attack from range. Watch your ammunition and your pet's level when you tame a new one.

Does my race matter?

Much less than your class. Pick the look you like and the faction your friends are on. Race does decide which classes you can play, so check the table.

Which classes can tank?

Warriors, Paladins and Druids. Blizzard also says an Enhancement Shaman can tank small groups for a while.

Which classes can heal?

Priests, Paladins, Shamans and Druids.

What are the new race and class combinations?

Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Gnome Priest, Orc Mage, Undead Paladin and Troll Warlock, plus the new Skyborne race.

Can I change my mind later?

Not on the same character: your class is for good. Everyone starts at level 1 in Forever, and there is no character boost.
