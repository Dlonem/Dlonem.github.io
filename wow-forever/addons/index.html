Best WoW Forever add-ons

The add-ons worth installing for World of Warcraft: Forever, from the five I'd call must-haves to the ones for what you do in the game: guild tools, leveling, tanking, healing, damage, crafting and world PvP. Every one has a Forever version. Where the add-ons folder is, what the game already does without them, and what I left out.

If you only read one thing

The short version

You don't need a single add-on to play Forever. Install the ones that fix something that bugs you, and leave the rest.

THOMAS, DLONEM

The five I'd call must-haves: iRC: Guild Connect if you are in a guild, DungeonJournal for dungeons, GearQuest for upgrades, Auctionator for gold, and Leatrix Plus for the small annoyances. Everything below is sorted by what you do, and the buttons show only the add-ons for one kind of player.

I play

Every add-on here had a Forever version on CurseForge when I checked on 26 October 2026, except HonorBound, which lives on GitHub. Add-ons get updated, renamed and abandoned, so check the page before you install.

Before anything else

Setting up add-ons for Forever

Forever is its own game in the Battle.net app, so it has its own add-ons folder. Nothing from retail, Classic or the beta is used by it.

World of Warcraft\ ├─ _forever_\ WoW Forever │ ├─ Interface\ │ │ └─ AddOns\ one folder per add-on │ └─ WTF\ settings and saved add-on data ├─ _classic_beta_\ the Forever beta, if it is still installed └─ _retail_\ retail WoW, with its own add-ons
  1. Start Forever once. Get to character select and quit, so the game makes its folders.
  2. With the CurseForge app: pick World of Warcraft, choose WoW Forever as the game version, and install from there. It finds the Forever folder and only offers Forever files.
  3. By hand: download the Forever file, unzip it into _forever_\Interface\AddOns, and check you have AddOns\NameOfTheAddon, not AddOns\Interface\AddOns\NameOfTheAddon. A folder nested one level too deep is the most common reason an add-on does not show up.
  4. Restart the game fully and check the AddOns button at character select. Anything that is unticked there is not loaded.
  5. Coming from the beta? The beta used _classic_beta_. Install your add-ons again from their Forever versions rather than copying the beta's AddOns folder. If you want an add-on's old settings, copy its file from the beta's WTF folder into the same place under _forever_.

One manager per folder. If you use CurseForge, don't also point another add-on manager at the same folder: two of them updating the same add-ons get in each other's way.

Already in the game

What Forever does without add-ons

A few jobs that needed an add-on in Classic are in the game now, which is why some old favorites are optional here:

  • Quest objectives and turn-ins. The map and the quest tracker show where to go for the quests in your log. Quests you have not picked up yet are not shown; that is the gap Questie fills.
  • A damage meter and a cooldown manager, from Blizzard.
  • Group Finder, to find people for a dungeon. It does not build the group for you.

Blizzard has said Forever shares retail World of Warcraft's interface code and its limits on add-ons, so add-ons can change how the game looks and what it tells you, not play it for you.

Play together

Guild tools

If you are in a guild, these are the two worth having. The first one only works if most of the guild runs it, so it is worth asking your officers.

Guild

iRC: Guild Connect

A companion add-on for guilds. It shares everyone's professions and recipes, gives the guild a home page with its rules, and has an optional guild map that shows where members are out in the world.

  • The guild master switches the map on, and you only see members who run the add-on too.
  • Officers get roster tools, welcome messages, and a browser of other guilds and their leaderboards.

The Grey Company runs this one. It is the add-on I ask every member to install.

Leveling and questing

Leveling add-ons fall into two groups: guides that tell you where to go next, and helpers that make the map and quest windows nicer. You need at most one guide.

Leveling

RestedXP Guide

A step-by-step leveling route with an arrow pointing to the next step, and automatic quest accept and turn-in.

  • The starting zones are free: up to level 20 on the Alliance and 22 on the Horde.
  • The routes after that are sold on the RestedXP website.

CurseForge →

Leveling

Guidelime

A free leveling guide. It ticks off quest progress and kills by itself where it can, and an arrow points to the next step.

  • Guides are separate add-ons. TUGs' guide pack lists Forever and covers both factions.
  • Its code is public on GitHub, but its licence is all rights reserved, so it is free rather than open source.

The free answer to RestedXP.

Questie

Marks quest givers, objectives and turn-ins on your map and minimap, including quests you have not picked up yet.

  • Forever already shows objectives and turn-ins for the quests in your log. What Questie adds is the quests you have not found yet.

Skip it if half the fun for you is finding quests yourself.

Crafting and gold

Professions are a bigger deal in Forever than in Classic: Blizzard's developers put the number of new recipes at over 600. These make leveling them, and selling what you make, much less work.

Crafting

On any ruleset

World PvP

How much world PvP you see depends on your ruleset. On PvP you are flagged in contested zones whether you like it or not. On Normal you only fight other players if you turn PvP on yourself: type /pvp, or right-click your portrait. Blizzard announced Roleplay-PvP on 22 October 2026 and has not published its rules yet. The ruleset guide has the details.

I play on Normal and turn PvP on when I feel like a fight. These three make that more than wandering around hoping:

World PvP

War Effort

Everyone running it is a scout. Enemy players you see are shared with your guild and faction and drawn live on the map, with an alert when they come close.

  • Only enemies with PvP on count, and only in the open world, so it is busier on the PvP ruleset than on Normal.
  • Scout ranks, daily contracts and a guild leaderboard, and it works with Overlord Forever.

I run it. It turns a quiet Normal evening into a reason to go and look.

CurseForge →

World PvPGuild

Overlord Forever

A player-run war for the open world: capture points on seven fronts, Southshore against Tarren Mill among them, guild keeps and outposts, and weekly rankings.

  • Normal, PvP, Roleplaying and Hardcore keep separate standings.
  • You need PvP on to capture a point.

CurseForge →

World PvP

HonorBound

Swear an oath and it keeps your character flagged for PvP. If anything turns your flag off, your next key press turns it back on. Sworn players show a crest you can see if you run it too.

  • Stay unflagged for five minutes and the oath breaks.
  • It is not on CurseForge. Download it from GitHub and unzip it into your AddOns folder.
  • It was written when Forever had no Roleplay-PvP ruleset. On Normal it is a way to stay in the fight on purpose.

And why

What I left out

WeakAuras. The add-on most people ask about, and it has no Forever version. Its team said in October 2025 that it would not make a version for retail's Midnight expansion, because Midnight's new add-on limits took away too much of what it does. Forever shares those limits. Its built-in cooldown manager covers part of the job.

ElvUI. It has never been on CurseForge; it comes from the Tukui site. In October, the sites that track Forever add-ons disagreed on whether its current release supports Forever, so check Tukui's own page before you build a UI on it.

Zygor Guides. Paid, and not listed with a Forever version. RestedXP and Guidelime cover the same job.

Add-ons with a handful of users. There are lots of brand new Forever add-ons. I left out the ones whose own authors call them an early test, such as ForeverCallToWar, and the ones nobody seems to use yet. TrueshotLodge is the exception, because nothing bigger does its job.

If you think something belongs on this list, tell me in the guild Discord.

When something breaks

Keeping add-ons healthy

  • Update after every patch. An add-on built for the last version can throw errors after an update. Let your manager update everything before you log in.
  • Errors at login? At character select, open AddOns, untick everything, and turn them back on a few at a time until the error comes back. The last one you turned on is the culprit. BugSack names the add-on behind an error, which saves most of the guessing.
  • Two add-ons, one job. Two nameplate add-ons, or two boss mods, fight over the same thing. Keep one.
  • Only use the Forever file. A retail or Classic file in the Forever folder may load and then break.
Quick answers

Questions

What are the must-have add-ons for WoW Forever?

None is required: Forever plays fine without add-ons. The five I'd install first are iRC: Guild Connect if you are in a guild, DungeonJournal for dungeons, GearQuest for upgrades, Auctionator for gold, and Leatrix Plus for the small annoyances. After that, pick by role: the filter at the top of this page shows the ones for yours.

Do add-ons work in WoW Forever?

Yes. Blizzard confirmed add-ons for Forever. It shares retail World of Warcraft's interface code and the same limits on what add-ons may do, so an add-on needs a Forever version: a retail or Classic version on its own is not enough.

Where is the WoW Forever add-ons folder?

In your World of Warcraft folder, under _forever_\Interface\AddOns. Each add-on is its own folder inside AddOns. If the folder is not there yet, start Forever once and get to character select, then quit.

Why are my add-ons not showing up in WoW Forever?

Usually one of three things: the add-on is in another game's folder (it has to be _forever_\Interface\AddOns, not _retail_ or _classic_beta_), it is nested one folder too deep, or it is unticked in the AddOns list at character select. A retail or Classic file in place of the Forever one can also fail to load.

Did my beta add-ons carry over?

No. The beta kept its own folder, _classic_beta_. Install the Forever versions of your add-ons again. If you want an add-on's old settings, copy its file from the beta's WTF folder into the same place under _forever_.

Do I need Questie in WoW Forever?

Not to finish quests: Forever already marks objectives and turn-ins for the quests in your log. Questie adds the quests you have not picked up yet, which the game does not show.

Does WoW Forever have a built-in damage meter?

Yes, and a cooldown manager. Details! is still worth it if you want breakdowns by spell and by fight.

Does WeakAuras work in WoW Forever?

No. It has no Forever version. Its team said it would not make one for retail's Midnight expansion because of Midnight's new add-on limits, and Forever shares those limits. The cooldown manager built into Forever covers part of what it did.

Does ElvUI work in WoW Forever?

Check before you count on it. ElvUI comes from the Tukui site rather than CurseForge, and in October the sites that track Forever add-ons disagreed on whether its current release supports Forever. Tukui's own page is the one to trust.

Can I use CurseForge and another add-on manager together?

Better not on the same folder. Two managers updating the same add-ons get in each other's way. Pick one.

Which add-ons does your guild use?

iRC: Guild Connect, for everyone in The Grey Company. The rest is up to each player; I also run War Effort for world PvP.
