The scoring

How it works

Each answer adds points to the classes it fits, usually three or four of them, and a few answers take a point away. A second answer on the same question counts half, which is how the quiz tells a Paladin who also likes the wild from a Druid who also likes the Light. At the end, every class gets a match: the points it earned divided by the most it could have earned from the questions you answered. That way a class that shows up in lots of answers does not win just by showing up.

The weights come from what each class does in Forever, taken from Blizzard's class pages and deep dives and the cards in the class guide. Before it went up, I wrote 28 sets of answers as different kinds of player, from a born tank and a dedicated healer to a Paladin who just wants to hit things, and checked that every one lands on the class it should. The site's checks run them again every time the page is rebuilt.

The spec suggestion follows from your answers too: say you want to keep people alive and you land on a Shaman, it suggests Restoration. Talents start at level 10 and a trainer can reset them, so treat it as a place to start.

What it can't know: what your friends play, which faction they are on, and which race you like the look of. For those, see the race table and the ruleset guide.

One line each

The nine classes

The roles are the ones Blizzard's class pages describe. Each name opens its full card.

  • Hunter Damage — A pet holds the enemy while you attack from range, which makes it the easiest class to understand.
  • Warrior TankDamage — Blizzard says a Warrior fits any group as a tank or a damage dealer.
  • Paladin TankHealerDamage — Heavy armor and healing of your own, and it can tank, heal or deal damage.
  • Warlock Damage — Ranged damage with a demon pet at your side, which helps a lot when you play alone.
  • Mage Damage — Strong single-target and area damage, Polymorph for crowd control, and you make your own food and water.
  • Priest HealerDamage — Blizzard calls it the most versatile healer in the game. Shadow is its damage spec.
  • Shaman HealerDamage — Melee or spell damage, or healing, in mail with a shield, and totems that help the whole group.
  • Rogue Damage — Melee damage, with stealth to pick your fights.
  • Druid TankHealerDamage — Blizzard says a Druid can fill any role with the right gear and talents.
Quick answers

Questions

Which class should I play in WoW Forever?

The one that fits how you like to play. If you are new or casual, I would start with a Hunter, then a Warrior or Paladin. The quiz on this page asks fifteen questions about how you play and gives a best fit, a spec to start with, and the classes close behind.

What is the easiest class in WoW Forever for a new player?

Hunter. A pet holds the enemy while you attack from range, which makes it the easiest class to understand. Warrior and Paladin are the next two I would point a new player at.

Which class is best for leveling alone?

A class with a pet has the easiest time, because the pet takes the hits: Hunter, or Warlock with a demon at its side. The quiz's last question asks how you will mostly play, and weighs exactly this.

How accurate is the class quiz?

It is tested against 28 answer sets written as different kinds of player, from a born tank to a player torn between Paladin and Druid, and every one lands on the class it should. Your result is still a starting point: read the class card before you commit.

Why doesn't the quiz ask if I want a pet or a bow?

Because then you would already know the answer. Every question asks about the feel of play, and every answer leans toward several classes at once, so no single answer decides it.

Can I pick two answers?

Yes. Tap your pick, then tap a second answer that also fits and it counts half. It is for the questions where two things are true of you, such as liking both holy light and the wild. One pick per question is still fine.

Can I skip a question?

Yes. A skipped question counts for and against no class. You need at least eight answers for a result.

Does the quiz save my answers?

Only in the page's address, so you can share or reload your result. Nothing is sent anywhere or stored.

Does race matter?

Much less than class. The result lists which races can play your class on each faction, and the class guide has the full race table.
