City of Dalaran

Dalaran is back on the ground, and its streets are a dungeon. How Alliance gets the sewer key, the four quests worth picking up, all eight bosses in the order we ran them, and the secret one most groups walk straight past. From two runs I recorded on 16 November.

If you only read one thing

The short version

The door is a locked sewer grate on the west side of Dalaran. For Alliance the key is one quest: An Alarming Request, from Southshore.

  • Two quests in Stormwind first. A Green Sample (the herbalism trainer) and Power Overwhelming (the top of the mage tower). Both open at level 24.
  • Two more at the grate. Heart of Disruption and Starving Arcane come from Modera once you have the key. Both need level 30.
  • Don't skip the secret boss. If someone has a Tome of Dalaran, summon Lyn the Ignored in the sewers before you go up to the streets.
  • Once you're on the streets, the order is up to you. The last boss needs you to talk to Archmage Modera in the Violet Citadel first.

Getting in

The Dalaran Sewer Key

The dungeon is behind a sewer grate, and the grate reads Locked, Requires Dalaran Sewer Key until you have it.

  1. Southshore Town Hall. Pick up An Alarming Request from Emissary Jacques of the Kirin Tor, around 48, 60.
  2. Walk northwest to Dalaran. Follow the road around the west side of the city.
  3. The sewer grate. Turn the quest in to the Image of Archmage Modera, around 68, 45. The Dalaran Sewer Key goes on your keyring, and Modera offers Heart of Disruption and Starving Arcane.

The grate is in Silverpine Forest on the map, not Alterac. The written guides say one key lets the whole group in; my footage doesn't show it either way, so it's worth everyone doing the quest.

Eight fights

The bosses

The order is the one we ran on the second run. Once you are up on the streets, the order is up to you. Mechanics are from Warcraft Tavern's guide; names and places are checked against my two runs.

Show tips for
1

Atrexis the Grave Knight

Necromancer · the sewers
Summon SkeletonsUnholy Frenzy
  • The first boss, not even 30 seconds into the dungeon. Clear the undead around him before you pull.
  • He raises skeletons during the fight.
2

Lyn the Ignored

Secret · summoned in the sewers
Summon SkeletonsCurse of Thorns
  • Only there if you summon him with a Tome of Dalaran. How to do it.
  • Curse of Thorns goes on you.
  • Moderate damage on the whole group, on top of the skeletons.

Bonepile Gaze dropped for us on the first run: a shield with 718 armor that needs level 28. Nothing notable on the second.

3

Arcane Anomaly

Arcane elemental · where Krasus' Landing used to be
Focal Blast
  • Focal Blast is a frontal beam that rotates. Stay close to the boss and it's easy to step around.
  • The first boss up on the streets. If you're here, you have gone past Lyn the Ignored.
4

Mana Devourer

Mana serpent · the Violet Hold
DebilitateDrain Vitality
  • Clear the mobs in the hold first.
  • Drain Vitality is damage over time. Heal through it.
5

Fel Ancient

Corrupted ancient · Runeweaver Square
EntangleFel Timbers
  • Move out of the Fel Timbers on the ground. Anything that breaks roots helps.
  • The Fel Infused Blossom for A Green Sample is here.
6

Unstable Sentinel

Golem · Magus Commerce Exchange
Malfunction
  • Malfunction is heavy damage around it. Move away when it casts.
  • Warcraft Tavern calls it the Unstable Sentry; the name over its health bar in my run read Unstable Sentinel.
7

Arcanic Enigma

Arcane elemental · Path of Renewal
PolymorphSilenceArcane Manalings
  • Kill the two Mana Phantoms with it first.
  • It polymorphs party members and silences everyone nearby.
  • Power Overwhelming wants this one.
8

Shade of the Archmage

The Violet Chamber
Bounding ManaMass Polymorph
  • Talk to Archmage Modera inside the Violet Citadel; she opens the portal to the Violet Chamber.
  • Step away from Bounding Mana, a slow projectile. When he runs out of mana he casts Mass Polymorph; break it as soon as it lands.
  • Loot the Arcane Mote for Heart of Disruption.
The boss you can walk past

Summoning Lyn the Ignored

Lyn the Ignored is not in the dungeon until you summon him, so most groups never know he is there. If your group has reached the Arcane Anomaly up on the streets, you have already gone past him.

  1. Get a Tome of Dalaran. It starts as the Black Tome in Shadowfang Keep and becomes the Tome of Dalaran at the Ambermill Leyline Focus. How to get it. The Black Tome itself doesn't work in Dalaran.
  2. Take the passage across the room from the first boss, Atrexis.
  3. Clear the side room.
  4. Stand on the purple circle and use the Tome.

Both tomes are soulbound and unique, so whoever carries one can keep it in the bank until the group is ready.

Quick answers

Questions

How do I get the Dalaran Sewer Key as Alliance?

One quest. Pick up An Alarming Request from Emissary Jacques in the Southshore Town Hall, around 48, 60. Walk northwest to Dalaran, follow the road around the west side of the city, and turn it in to the Image of Archmage Modera at the sewer grate, around 68, 45. The key goes on your keyring.

What level is City of Dalaran?

Blizzard's dungeon post says 28 to 33. The two quests at the sewer grate need level 30; the two from Stormwind open at 24.

How many bosses are in City of Dalaran?

Eight, one of them secret: Atrexis the Grave Knight, Lyn the Ignored, Arcane Anomaly, Mana Devourer, Fel Ancient, Unstable Sentinel, Arcanic Enigma and the Shade of the Archmage.

How do I summon Lyn the Ignored?

With a Tome of Dalaran, which starts as the Black Tome in Shadowfang Keep. In the sewers, take the passage across the room from the first boss, clear the side room, stand on the purple circle and use the Tome. If your group has reached the Arcane Anomaly on the streets, you have already gone past him.

Can I use the Black Tome in Dalaran?

No. It has to become the Tome of Dalaran first, at the Ambermill Leyline Focus in Silverpine Forest.

Can Starving Arcane be done in one run?

It wasn't for me. It wants 8 Seeking Remnants, 8 Saturated Remnants and 8 Mana Echoes, and my first run ended at 5, 2 and 5.

How long does City of Dalaran take?

Both of my runs took 32 minutes.
