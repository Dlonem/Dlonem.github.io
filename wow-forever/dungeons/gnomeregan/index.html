WoW Forever dungeon guide

Gnomeregan

Not one of the nine new dungeons, but Forever kept every quest and changed the rewards. Gnomeregan has more quests than almost any dungeon in the game, and three of them start before you zone in. All nine for Alliance, the punch cards in order, and the door most people never open. From my run on 16 November.

If you only read one thing

The short version

Do three things before you zone in: grab Gnogaine from Ozzie Togglevolt in Kharanos, kill Techbot outside the instance portal, and take a White Punch Card from the mobs outside.

  • The punch cards go A, B, C, D. Machine A is outside the entrance. Miss one and you're walking back.
  • Clean the ring before you leave. The Grime-Encrusted Ring from the Dark Iron Agents goes in the Sparklematic 5200.
  • Don't fall during the escort. A Fine Mess fails if you do. Mine did.
  • Keep the Workshop Key. It opens a shortcut from deep in the dungeon back to the entrance.

Warcraft Tavern says Forever kept Gnomeregan's quests from Classic and changed the rewards.

The punch cards, in order

Data Rescue wants a Prismatic Punch Card, and you only get it by running one card through four machines in order. The first card and the first machine are both outside the dungeon, so start there.

Most people never use it

The Workshop Key and the back door

Electrocutioner 6000 drops the Workshop Key. It opens a workshop door in the Engineering Labs that leads straight back out to the tunnels by the entrance, next to the first punch card machine.

  • Leaving: you can walk out from deep in the dungeon instead of back through it. That is what I did.
  • Coming back: the same door is a way back in halfway through. My video only shows it from the inside going out.
In the order we met them

The bosses

  1. Grubbis.
  2. Viscous Fallout. Two bosses down took us about 36 minutes.
  3. Electrocutioner 6000, with the Workshop Key.
  4. Crowd Pummeler.
  5. Mekgineer Thermaplugg, the last boss, for The Grand Betrayal.

Quick answers

Questions

How many quests does Gnomeregan have for Alliance in WoW Forever?

Nine: Save Techbot's Brain!, Gnogaine (which leads to The Only Cure is More Green Glow), Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators, Essential Artificials, Data Rescue, A Fine Mess, The Sparklematic 5200!, Gnome Improvement and The Grand Betrayal.

What order do the punch card machines go in?

White card from the mobs outside. Then machine A outside the entrance turns it yellow, B in the Dormitory blue, C in the Launch Bay red, and D in the Engineering Labs prismatic.

What does the Gnomeregan Workshop Key do?

It drops from Electrocutioner 6000 and opens a workshop door in the Engineering Labs that leads straight back out to the tunnels by the entrance, next to the first punch card machine.

Where does A Fine Mess turn in?

To Scooty in Booty Bay, after you escort Kernobee out. Don't fall during the escort; I did, and it failed.

What do I do with the Grime-Encrusted Ring?

Clean it at the Sparklematic 5200 in the Clean Zone before you leave the dungeon.
