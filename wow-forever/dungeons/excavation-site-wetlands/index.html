Excavation Site: Wetlands

A titan dig above Whelgar's Excavation, and the one new dungeon whose entrance fools almost everyone. Where the portal really is, the four quests to pick up before you go in, all seven Alliance quests, the chain behind the optional boss, and the four bosses. If you only read one thing

The short version

The entrance is not the cave. The meeting stone is at Thelgen Rock (53, 65). Skip the cave next to it, take the trail up the hill beside the stone, and the portal is at the top (49, 56).

  • Pick up four quests first. Highland Hides and Lost in the Thicket Things in Menethil Harbor, Songblade Search in Lakeshire, and Horrors in the Highland from Rethiel the Greenwarden once his chain is done.
  • Bring a priest or a paladin. Our tank picked up four stacks of Muculent Rot in the Lost Marsh. It is a disease, it lasts 30 minutes, and our druid could not remove it.
  • Ask about the Highland Horror before you start. It is an optional boss and groups often skip it, but Horrors in the Highland needs it.
  • Plan on about 45 minutes. Our run took 42, and five or six of those went to a death.

Thelgen Rock

Getting there

Thelgen Rock is in the middle of the Wetlands, on the Loch Modan side. Alliance can follow the road from Menethil Harbor.

  1. Find the meeting stone at Thelgen Rock, around 53, 65.
  2. Don't take the cave. It is right next to the stone and looks like the way in. It isn't.
  3. Take the trail up the hill beside the stone. There are a few spiders on the way.
  4. The portal is at the top, around 49, 56. Horde use the same portal, so expect company.

Warcraft Tavern puts the start of the trail at 53.9, 65.4 and the portal at 48.1, 56.5, which matches my minimap to within a step or two.

The optional boss

Rethiel's chain

Horrors in the Highland is the only Excavation Site quest with real setup. Rethiel the Greenwarden won't give it to you until you have done his whole chain. I ran it solo at level 29 with a timer running.

Warcraft Tavern lists the gnolls that drop Crude Flints as Mosshide Brutes, Trappers, Fenrunners, Mistweavers and Alphas. A second player cuts Fire Taboo down a lot. Rethiel also offers Carnage once the chain is done; that one isn't a dungeon quest.

Finding the Fen Creepers

Blisters on the Land is an old quest that people have been stuck on for years, because the map sends you to water that looks empty. The creepers are level 25 and stealthed in the streams. Four things worked for me:

  1. Run through the water. If you are a few levels above them, they pop out when you get close.
  2. Listen. You hear the stealth sound before you see one.
  3. Check where the streams meet. Another player told me that, and it was right.
  4. Do another quest in the same water. I did Apprentice's Duties (the Giant Crocolisk Skins) while I looked and finished both together.

One spot in Sundown Marsh, around 22, 25, spawned two at once. All twelve took me about 17 minutes.

The route

One map, four areas, in the order you reach them. Area names are the ones on the minimap.

  1. 1The Lost MarshSaltspine, a giant crocolisk, patrols here. This is where our tank picked up Muculent Rot.
  2. 2Stalker's ThicketRaptors, and the Thicket Raptor Hides for Highland Hides. Shadetooth.
  3. 3The Lost Dig SiteDaewyn's tent for Songblade Search. The optional Highland Horror is north of it.
  4. 4The Site of the GuardianArdin Grassman on the wooden platform, then the Relic Guardian.

The bosses

Boss mechanics are from Warcraft Tavern's guide; the names, places and drops are checked against my own run.

Show tips for
1

Saltspine

Crocolisk · The Lost Marsh
Tendon RipDust Storm
  • A giant crocolisk that patrols the opening marsh, so watch where it is before you pull the packs around it.
  • Tendon Rip slows you. Dust Storm lowers everyone's chance to hit, so expect a slower fight than the health bar suggests.
  • Muculent Rot comes from this area too. It is a disease; dispel it if you can.

Shadetooth

Raptor · Stalker's Thicket
FearThicket HuntersEnrage
  • Hidden raptors in the grass call for help when attacked. Clear slowly.
  • He fears everyone around him and brings Thicket Hunter adds.
  • He enrages at low health. Save cooldowns for the end.

Highland Horror

Optional · north of the Lost Dig Site
Entangling RootsWild Regeneration
  • Optional. Only players on Horrors in the Highland need it, so groups often skip it. Ask before the run starts.
  • Interrupt Wild Regeneration. It heals him.
  • His Entangling Roots catches the whole group.

Drops the Horrible Rootcore for Horrors in the Highland.

Relic Guardian

Titan construct · Site of the Guardian
ThunderclapKnockback

  • Thunderclap, and an occasional knockback on you.

Quick answers

Questions

Where is the Excavation Site: Wetlands entrance?

Not in the cave at Thelgen Rock. The meeting stone is at Thelgen Rock, around 53, 65. Take the trail up the hill beside the stone, clear a few spiders, and the portal is at the top, around 49, 56.

What level is Excavation Site: Wetlands?

Blizzard's dungeon post says 24 to 29. Warcraft Tavern and Icy Veins tune it at 26 to 33, and the quests open at level 24. I ran it at 29.

How many quests does Excavation Site: Wetlands have for Alliance?

Seven. Four you pick up before you go in (Highland Hides, Lost in the Thicket Things, Horrors in the Highland and Songblade Search), and three start inside: Fallen in the Fen, Heartwoven and the Titan Relic from the last boss.

How do I get Horrors in the Highland?

From Rethiel the Greenwarden in The Green Belt, around 56, 40, after his chain: The Greenwarden, Tramping Paws, Fire Taboo and Blisters on the Land. The three quests before it took me 58 minutes solo at level 29.

Where are the Fen Creepers for Blisters on the Land?

In the streams of the Wetlands, stealthed. Run through the water, listen for the stealth sound, and check where streams meet. One spot in Sundown Marsh, around 22, 25, spawned two for me.

Is the Highland Horror optional?

Yes. It only matters to players on Horrors in the Highland, so a lot of groups walk past it. Tell your group you need it before the run starts.

Can a druid cure Muculent Rot?

Not in our run. Our tank picked up four stacks in the Lost Marsh; it is a disease that lasts 30 minutes, and our druid could not remove it. Bring a priest or a paladin.
