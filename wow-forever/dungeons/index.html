Forever's rules

What's different about dungeons

  • Every dungeon drop was looked at again. Blizzard says it re-examined every item, and a boss's unique items are now blue (rare) quality.
  • Shared appearances. When an Uncommon or Rare item that binds on pickup drops, its appearance goes to everyone eligible in the group, not only the person who wins it.
  • Recipes from bosses. Advanced Blueprint recipes for professions drop from dungeon bosses.
  • Crowd control matters. Blizzard wants dungeons to stay tactical, where crowd control can be the right answer to a pull.
  • Your ruleset is your pool. You can only group with players on the same ruleset, so pick the one your friends play on.

From Blizzard's Deep Dive panel recap.

Finding a group

Group Finder

To list yourself you pick a playstyle: Learning, Relaxed, Competitive or Carry Offered. Group Finder finds the people; it does not build the group for you or teleport anyone, so everyone still walks to the entrance.

If it's your first time through a dungeon, Learning is the right pick.
