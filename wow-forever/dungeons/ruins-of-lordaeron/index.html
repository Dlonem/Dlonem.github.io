Ruins of Lordaeron

The ruined capital above the Undercity, and a dungeon you can wander instead of following a corridor. The two things in it that aren't on any map: the hidden boss behind a fireplace, and a crest that moves. From my run as an Alliance druid on 7 November.

If you only read one thing

The short version

Blizzard's post calls it a ruined city that Forsaken forces have to secure against a necromancer building power. It sits on the surface, in the ruins above the Undercity in Tirisfal Glades, and Method describes it as non-linear: there's no single path through it.

  • The hidden boss is behind a fireplace in an abandoned house west of the Baron. Five waves, then Viktor.
  • The Crest of Lordaeron has no marker and spawns in one of five places. Check each one as you pass it instead of backtracking like we did.
  • Alliance can run it. I did, as a druid, and the Crest turns in at Stormwind Keep.

The hidden boss

Viktor the Vile

Viktor the Vile isn't on the map, and he isn't standing anywhere. You light him.

  1. After the Baron, follow the southern road west to the abandoned house by the purple blight, next to the round tower.
  2. Clear the area around the house first.
  3. Click the fireplace. In my run it read Warm Kindling.
  4. Survive five waves of undead. Watch your healer's mana.
  5. Then Viktor.

People search for him as Victor the Vile; the game spells it with a k.

No marker

The Crest of Lordaeron

The Crest of Lordaeron starts a quest when you right-click it, and it can be in any of a few places. We checked three before we found it. Check each spot as you pass it:

  1. The crypt just outside the Baron.
  2. The top of the tower by the entrance.
  3. The top of the tower by the Abandoned.
  4. Behind the banshee door in the spider courtyard.
  5. The back wall of Bjork's room. That is where ours was.

Turn it in to Lady Dena Kennedy in the west wing of Stormwind Keep, not at the tram, whatever the quest marker says.

The reward was a Small Sack of Gems and a big chunk of experience. The list of spots comes from my run and the comments on Wowhead's quest page.

Six fights

The bosses

Warcraft Tavern and Method both list six bosses:

  • Witherfang
  • The Baron
  • Viktor the Vile, the summoned one
  • The Abandoned
  • Bjork
  • Rath'mael

Because the dungeon is open, the order is up to your group. My videos cover the two things in it that aren't on any map: Viktor and the Crest.

Quick answers

Questions

How do I summon Viktor the Vile?

After the Baron, follow the southern road west to the abandoned house by the purple blight, next to the round tower. Click the fireplace to start the event. Five waves of undead come first, then Viktor.

Is it Viktor or Victor the Vile?

Viktor, with a k. That is how the game spells it when you kill him.

Where is the Crest of Lordaeron?

It has no map marker and spawns in one of a few spots: the crypt just outside the Baron, the top of the tower by the entrance, the top of the tower by the Abandoned, behind the banshee door in the spider courtyard, or on the back wall of Bjork's room. Ours was in Bjork's room.

Where do I turn in the Crest of Lordaeron?

To Lady Dena Kennedy in the west wing of Stormwind Keep, not the tram, whatever the quest marker says.

What level is Ruins of Lordaeron?

Blizzard's dungeon post says 15 to 20.

Can Alliance do Ruins of Lordaeron?

Yes. The dungeon is built around the Forsaken, but I ran it as an Alliance druid, and the Crest of Lordaeron quest turns in at Stormwind Keep.
