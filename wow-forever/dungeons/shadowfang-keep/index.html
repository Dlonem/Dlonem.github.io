Shadowfang Keep

Shadowfang Keep is a long way from anywhere for Alliance. Forever added a boat from Stormwind Harbor, and that is where the route starts now. The way there, and the book in Arugal's room that you'll want later. From my trip on 10 November.

The short version

  • Two boats. Stormwind Harbor to Darkshore, then the Wetlands boat, and stay on it to Southshore.
  • Take the Southshore flight path and you never need the boats again.
  • Nothing to pick up. There are no Alliance quests inside.
  • Take the Black Tome. It's on the alchemy table in Arugal's room, and it is the key to the secret boss in City of Dalaran.

Stormwind to the keep

The Alliance route

  1. Stormwind Harbor. Take the boat to Darkshore. Method's travel guide says this boat is new in Forever.
  2. Darkshore. Walk to the other dock and take the boat to the Wetlands.
  3. Stay on the boat. It stops at Menethil Harbor and carries on to Southshore in Hillsbrad Foothills.
  4. Southshore. Take the flight path.
  5. Walk west. Follow the road into Silverpine Forest and turn right up the hill to the keep.

My trip took 22 minutes, and most of it was waiting: each boat took over three minutes to arrive. On a PvP ruleset, stay off the road through Hillsbrad and cut through the woods.

Take it with you

The Black Tome

There is a book in Shadowfang Keep that most groups walk past. It's the start of the summon for Lyn the Ignored, the secret boss in City of Dalaran (levels 28 to 33).

  1. Kill Archmage Arugal, the last boss.
  2. Loot the Black Tome from the alchemy table in his room, up the stairs at the back.
  3. Run east to Ambermill in Silverpine Forest, around 63, 64.
  4. Use it at the Leyline Focus. Go into the town hall at the back, stand next to the blue crystal beside Archmage Ataeric, and use the Black Tome from your bags. It becomes the Tome of Dalaran.

Both tomes are soulbound and unique, so keep it in your bank until you're high enough for Dalaran. Ambermill is held by Kirin Tor mages in Forever, and nothing there attacked me as Alliance. The other book in Arugal's room, the Dalaran Digest, is a separate collectible you turn in at Stormwind.

Quick answers

Questions

How do Alliance get to Shadowfang Keep in WoW Forever?

Take the boat from Stormwind Harbor to Darkshore, walk to the other dock and take the boat to the Wetlands. Stay on it when it stops at Menethil Harbor; it carries on to Southshore. Take the Southshore flight path, then follow the road west into Silverpine Forest and turn right up the hill to the keep.

How long does the trip take?

22 minutes door to door for me, and most of that was waiting on boats. Once you have the Southshore flight path you can skip them.

Where is the Black Tome?

On the alchemy table in Archmage Arugal's room, up the stairs at the back. Kill Arugal first, then loot it.

How do I turn the Black Tome into the Tome of Dalaran?

Take it to Ambermill in Silverpine Forest, around 63, 64. In the town hall at the back, stand next to the Leyline Focus beside Archmage Ataeric and use the Black Tome from your bags.

What is the Tome of Dalaran for?

It summons Lyn the Ignored, the secret boss in City of Dalaran.

Are there Alliance quests inside Shadowfang Keep?

No, so there is nothing to pick up before you go.
