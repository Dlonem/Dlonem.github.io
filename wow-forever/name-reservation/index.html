Name reservation

Early name reservation for World of Warcraft: Forever runs from 27 October to 3 November 2026. Who gets it, what you can do in that week, whether you need a new download, and how Forever's two-part names work, with pictures of my own beta character's name in game.

If you only read one thing

The short version

  • The window is 27 October to 3 November 2026. It opens at 1:00 p.m. PDT (4:00 p.m. EDT) in North America and 18:00 CET in Europe.
  • It comes with any Forever upgrade pack. A subscription on its own does not include it.
  • You can make up to three characters in total, across all rulesets. Names go to whoever takes them first.
  • Every character has a first and a second name, and the full name has to be unique in your region.
  • The characters are made in the game: install World of Warcraft: Forever, press Play and choose your ruleset. More on the download.
  • Everyone starts playing at the same time: 4 November 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST.

The upgrade packs

Who gets it

27 October to 3 November

How the window works

  1. Have an upgrade pack on the account you play on. If you have more than one World of Warcraft account, check which one the pack went to.
  2. Install World of Warcraft: Forever. It is a separate game from the World of Warcraft you may already have. The next section covers this.
  3. From 27 October, press Play and choose your ruleset. There is no realm list in Forever. Every character belongs to a ruleset (Normal, PvP, Roleplaying or Roleplay-PvP; Hardcore opens after release), you can only group with players on your own, and moving means a new character. The ruleset guide has a picker if you are undecided.
  4. Make your characters. Up to three in total, across all rulesets: race, class, looks, and a first and second name. Two on PvP leaves room for one on Normal or Roleplaying.
  5. Wait for release. Your names are held, and your characters enter the world with everyone else at 3:00 p.m. PST on 4 November.

What Blizzard has not said yet. I would rather tell you what is open than guess:

  • The hour the window closes on 3 November.
  • Whether the Skyborne can be made during the window, or only from release.
  • Whether Roleplay-PvP is in the list on the first day. Blizzard said on 22 October that it was working to make it so.
  • Whether deleting a reserved character frees its name for someone else.

Two things that were open on 13 October are now settled: the opening times above, and that you choose your ruleset when you press Play, before making the characters.

Hardcore is the one ruleset you can be sure is not in the window: Blizzard says it opens after release.

The client

Do I need a new download?

Yes, and plan on it being big. Forever is its own game in the Battle.net app, not a mode of the World of Warcraft you already have installed, and the release version is a separate entry from the beta.

  • It has its own entry. During the beta, the Game Version menu above the Play button listed "World of Warcraft: Forever Beta" under "In Development". For name reservation, Blizzard's steps are to pick "World of Warcraft: Forever" in that menu and install it. Retail World of Warcraft, Classic and Anniversary each keep their own install.
  • The beta is over. It ran from 17 September to 21 October. Its install is not the one you play, so you can remove it once the release version is in.
  • Name reservation happens inside the game. After the install, press Play, choose your ruleset, and the character creator opens.
  • Add-ons do not move across by themselves. The beta kept its own add-on folder. How to set up add-ons for Forever.
  • Clear the space now. Blizzard's World of Warcraft requirements ask for a solid state drive with 128 GB available. Blizzard has not published a separate figure for Forever, so that is the number to plan around.
  • Your beta character will not be waiting for you. Nothing Blizzard has posted says beta characters carry over.

Blizzard's own first step for the beta is worth repeating: update your graphics drivers before you install.

First name, second name

How names work in Forever

Every character in Forever has two names, like a first name and a family name. My beta Warlock was Dlonem Youtube: first name Dlonem, second name Youtube. Blizzard's rules for them:

  • The full name is unique within your region. North America, Europe and the other regions are each one pool, and there are no realms, so a name taken on one ruleset is taken on all of them.
  • First names can repeat. Two players can both be Asha, as long as their second names differ.
  • Second names can be shared. Blizzard's own example is three characters called Asha, Thalen and Mira Brightvale. A shared second name works as a family for your alts, or as a theme for a group of friends.
  • Both parts are checked together against the naming rules, so splitting a banned word across the two names does not get it through.
  • You can hide your second name in the world: Game Menu, Options, Nameplates, then the checkbox for My Secondary Name. It still shows in chat.
  • If a name is taken, try another combination, or use the random name generator in the character creator.

Picking names before the window. Write down your first choice for each character and two backups, so a taken name costs you seconds and not your spot. If you want matching second names across your characters or your friends, agree on them now: the second name is the part people will see on all of you.

Quick answers

Questions

When does WoW Forever name reservation start?

On 27 October 2026 at 1:00 p.m. PDT in North America (4:00 p.m. EDT) and 18:00 CET in Europe, and at 07:00 on 28 October in Korea and Taiwan. It runs until 3 November 2026. Blizzard has not published the hour it closes.

Who gets early name reservation?

Anyone with a WoW Forever upgrade pack: the Skyborne Heroic Pack, the Skyborne Epic Pack or the Warcraft Forever Collection. Blizzard says it is included with any upgrade purchase. A subscription on its own does not include it.

How many names can I reserve?

Up to three characters in total, across all rulesets. Blizzard's example: two characters on PvP leave room for one on Normal or Roleplaying. The limit only applies to the reservation window.

Can I reserve a name on the Roleplay-PvP ruleset?

Blizzard added a Roleplay-PvP ruleset on 22 October 2026 and said it was working to let you choose it and reserve names there from the start of the window. If it is not in the list when you press Play, it was not ready yet.

Can I play my reserved characters early?

Nothing Blizzard has posted says so. The game opens for everyone at 3:00 p.m. PST on 4 November 2026, and you need a subscription or Game Time to play from then.

Do I need to download WoW Forever again?

Yes. The beta was installed as "World of Warcraft: Forever Beta". For name reservation, Blizzard's steps are to pick "World of Warcraft: Forever" in the Game Version menu above the Play button, install it, press Play and choose your ruleset. Blizzard's requirements ask for an SSD with 128 GB available.

Is a name taken on one ruleset taken on all of them?

Yes. A full name is unique within your region, and there are no realms in Forever, so the same first and second name can only exist once in North America, once in Europe and so on, whatever the ruleset.

Can two characters share a first name?

Yes, as long as the second names differ. It is the full name that has to be unique.

Can I hide my second name?

In the game world, yes: Game Menu, Options, Nameplates, then the checkbox for My Secondary Name. It still shows in chat.

Does my beta character carry over?

Nothing Blizzard has posted says it does. Treat the beta as a test and plan to start fresh.
