Character names

First name, second name

How names work in Forever

Every character in Forever has two names, like a first name and a family name. My Warlock is Dlonem Youtube: first name Dlonem, second name Youtube. Blizzard's rules for them:

  • The full name is unique within your region. North America, Europe and the other regions are each one pool, and there are no realms, so a name taken on one ruleset is taken on all of them.
  • First names can repeat. Two players can both be Asha, as long as their second names differ.
  • Second names can be shared. Blizzard's own example is three characters called Asha, Thalen and Mira Brightvale. A shared second name works as a family for your alts, or as a theme for a group of friends.
  • Both parts are checked together against the naming rules, so splitting a banned word across the two names does not get it through.
  • You can hide your second name in the world: Game Menu, Options, Nameplates, then the checkbox for My Secondary Name. It still shows in chat.
When it's taken

Getting the name you want

  1. Have a backup ready. Write down your first choice and two others, first and second name each, before you open the character creator.
  2. Change the second name first. If your first name is taken, it is usually only taken with that second name. A different second name often gets it through.
  3. Agree second names with your friends. It is the part people see on all of you, and a shared one makes a group easy to spot.
  4. Stuck? The character creator has a random name generator.

Your ruleset doesn't change any of this. It does decide who you can play with, so pick it before you make the character.

October and November 2026

How name reservation worked

Before release, Blizzard let players with an upgrade pack make their characters early so the names were held. It is over, and this is the record of how it worked.

  • When: 27 October to 3 November 2026. It opened at 1:00 p.m. PDT in North America, 18:00 CET in Europe, and at 07:00 on 28 October in Korea and Taiwan.
  • Who: anyone with the Skyborne Heroic Pack, the Skyborne Epic Pack or the Warcraft Forever Collection. Blizzard called it "included with any upgrade purchase."
  • How many: up to three characters in total, across all rulesets, first come, first served.
  • How: in the game itself. You installed World of Warcraft: Forever, pressed Play, chose a ruleset and made the characters. Roleplay-PvP was added to the list on 22 October.
  • Then: everyone, reserved or not, started together at release, 3:00 p.m. PST on 4 November 2026.

The beta, from 17 September to 21 October 2026, had its own install, its own add-on folder and its own characters, and none of them carried over. Setting up add-ons for Forever.

Quick answers

Questions

How do names work in WoW Forever?

Every character has a first and a second name, like a first name and a family name. The full name has to be unique within your region, across every ruleset, because there are no realms.

Is a name taken on one ruleset taken on all of them?

Yes. The same first and second name can only exist once in North America, once in Europe and so on, whatever the ruleset.

Can two characters share a first name?

Yes, as long as the second names differ. It is the full name that has to be unique.

Can my alts share a second name?

Yes. Blizzard's own example is three characters called Asha, Thalen and Mira Brightvale. A shared second name works as a family for your alts, or as a theme for a group of friends.

Can I hide my second name?

In the game world, yes: Game Menu, Options, Nameplates, then the checkbox for My Secondary Name. It still shows in chat.

Can I still reserve a name?

No. Early name reservation ran from 27 October to 3 November 2026, before the game was released on 4 November. Today you take a name the normal way, by making a character.

When was WoW Forever name reservation?

From 27 October to 3 November 2026. It opened at 1:00 p.m. PDT in North America and 18:00 CET in Europe, and it came with any of the upgrade packs. Each account could make up to three characters across all rulesets.

Did beta characters carry over?

No. The beta, which ran from 17 September to 21 October 2026, had its own characters and its own install.
