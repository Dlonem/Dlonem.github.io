The loop

What you actually do

Fly, burn, hoard, upgrade, fly further. It's a simple loop that keeps opening up.

1

Take off

Send your dragon soaring across six living biomes. Dodge storms, weave through obstacles, and keep your breath up — every coin on the course is yours if you can reach it.

2

Breach the walls

Blast through siege walls, out-fly wild rival dragons, and hunt named ELITE bosses for rare Dragon Stones. Storm the Knight's Keep, herd a stampede of sheep, and rescue a princess whose Royal Favor pays you back.

3

Pour it into the hoard

Upgrade your Gold Vault, your goblins, your forge. Claim grander estates as you go and watch the gold rise across the whole scene — the treasury is the progress bar.

4

Grow the roost

Spend Dragon Stones on new dragons and dig into their perk trees. Pierce through obstacles, widen your breath, chain lightning, magnetize coins. Bond with a dragon and it literally grows.

5

Chase the leaderboard

The daily Royal Bounty shuffles a fresh course every morning — identical for every keeper — then shows you where you landed on the Realm Rankings.

How it treats you

A game that respects your time

Free to play, but not the kind of free-to-play that holds your dragons hostage.

What you get

  • The whole game, free — no paywalled chapters
  • Six biomes, the full roost, every perk tree
  • A daily Royal Bounty and the Realm Rankings
  • Progress that always feels earned, whether you play two minutes or an hour
  • Plays entirely offline — no connection needed
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in if you want cloud saves and achievements

What it doesn't do

  • No account, no sign-up, no email — progress saves on your device
  • No energy timers gating when you're allowed to play
  • No subscription
  • Ads are optional and the one-time Clear Skies purchase removes them for good
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us — we run no servers that could receive it

Read the privacy policy — it's short, because there isn't much to disclose.

Common questions

Before you fly

Is it actually free, or free-to-play free?

Free, with optional ads. The one-time Clear Skies purchase removes ads permanently, and it's also bundled into the Dragonlord's Boon. There are other optional purchases — gold and gem packs, starter bundles, Dragon Stone caches — but none of them gate content, and there's no subscription and no energy timer.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It's single-player and your save lives on your device, so it works on a plane or in a tunnel. If you want cloud saves, leaderboards and achievements you can optionally sign in to Google Play Games, and that copy lives in your own Google account — not ours.

Is it okay for kids?

It's a general-audience, family-friendly game — cartoon fantasy action, dragons breathing fire and knocking down castle walls. No account, no chat, no personal information required from anyone. Purchases are real money, though, so if you're handing a phone to a child it's worth switching on purchase authentication in the Play Store first.

How long does a run take?

About two minutes for a single flight. The meta layers — treasury upgrades, the roost, perk trees, estates — are what turn it into a longer session when you want one.

What are Dragon Stones for?

They're the rare currency, dropped mainly by named ELITE bosses, and they're what unlocks and deepens dragons. Hunting elites is the reliable way to grow the roost rather than just the vault.

Does the daily course differ between players?

No — the Royal Bounty shuffles one course each morning and it's the same sky for every keeper that day. That's what makes the Realm Rankings mean anything.
