The studio-wide version

Effective date: July 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 16, 2026

Dlonem LLC ("Dlonem," "we," "us," "our") builds the mobile applications Tiny Dragon Hoard and the Break Buddy apps (together, the "Apps"). This policy explains what information the Apps handle and how. We built our Apps to be privacy-friendly — most of them run entirely on your device.

Each app has its own support and privacy address, listed on that app’s policy page above — use it for anything specific to that app. For company-level questions, contact@dlonem.com reaches us too.

Information you enter stays on your device

The Break Buddy apps store what you enter — your tracking history, notes, and reminders — locally on your device. We do not collect it, send it to our servers, or have access to it. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

No accounts required

You can use the Apps without creating an account or giving us your name, email, or other personal details.

Advertising in the apps

Some Apps display ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain device and usage information, including advertising identifiers, under Google's own policies. You can read more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and manage ad personalization in your device settings. Apps that do not show ads do not collect this information.

Cookies and similar technologies on this website

This website (dlonem.com) sets no cookies of its own. It has no login, no shopping basket, and no first-party tracking. Where advertising is served on this site, however, advertising cookies set by third parties do apply, and the section below explains them.

Advertising on this website

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Turn off personalised ads from Google in Google Ads Settings (also reachable at My Ad Center).
  • Opt out of personalised advertising from many other vendors at aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so does not stop you seeing ads; it means the ones you see are less relevant.

Third-party vendors other than Google may also serve ads on this site. Where they do, you can visit their own websites to opt out of their use of cookies for personalised advertising. Google's own explanation of how it uses data from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it — including under the GDPR in the EU and UK — you will be asked for consent before any personalised advertising cookies are set, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — Google handles the transaction. See Google's Privacy Policy for how that information is handled.

Analytics

Neither our Apps nor this website use third-party analytics tracking. We do not run Google Analytics or any equivalent, and we do not build advertising or marketing profiles from anyone's visit. (If this changes in the future, we will update this policy to disclose it.)

Information sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share personal information with third parties except as needed to provide the services described above (for example, Google AdMob and Google Play Billing).

Children's privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Because your tracking data stays on your device, its security is tied to your device's own protections. We encourage you to use a device passcode and keep your software up to date.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@dlonem.com.
