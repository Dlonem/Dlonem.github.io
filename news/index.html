October 2026

  • 8 October 2026 · THC Break Buddy 1.0.52

    Android: a slip costs what the research supports

    A logged use now takes a dent sized to the dose instead of a reset, shown in points. One slip no longer restarts a regular user's whole drug test window, and the daily brief only says you're near baseline when you really are.

  • 8 October 2026 · Dlonem

    A beginner's guide to WoW Forever

    What returning players get wrong, which class to start with, how leveling, camping and professions work, Group Finder and Hall of Thanes, and Hardcore. Facts checked against Blizzard's own posts.

  • 8 October 2026 · Dlonem

    The Supernatural wiki goes up

    The whole mod on one page: every creature, every lifestyle perk tree by tree, the magic system with all 54 magic interactions and the four magic activities, the game rules, and the known issues.

  • 7 October 2026 · THC Break Buddy 1.0.51

    iOS: the September update arrives

    Everything Android got on 30 September: smart reminders on the days that are usually yours, a note the morning a badge unlocks, a new daily brief every day, and a break can be marked as quitting for good.

  • 4 October 2026 · Supernatural 2.37

    The Six Hides

    Six ancient wolf hides choose Champions of their own, and werewolves run in packs now, with an alpha, a Second and a Werewolf Gathering.

  • 1 October 2026 · Supernatural 2.36

    Crusader Kings III 1.20

    Supernatural runs on CK3 1.20: the creature doctrines live on rites, and your rite judges what you do once it knows what you are.

  • 1 October 2026 · The Grey Company

    The Grey Company

    A guild for World of Warcraft: Forever, which launches on 4 November. Alliance, Normal ruleset, NA servers, leveling and social, every time zone welcome. The Discord is open now, and dlonem.com/grey is the address to give people in game.

  • 1 October 2026 · Dlonem

    Engaged

    In September, on our trip to Italy, I asked my girlfriend of four years to marry me. She said yes.

September 2026

  • 30 September 2026 · THC Break Buddy 1.0.50

    Android: reminders that arrive once

    Reminders fire once, at the time you picked, in any time zone. Smart reminders give a heads-up on the days that are usually yours, the daily brief is new every day, and a break can now be marked as quitting for good.

  • 30 September 2026 · Nicotine Break Buddy 1.0.12

    The craving-window nudge works

    The nudge is timed to the trigger you told the app about. Reminders arrive once, at the time you picked, and badges earned overnight are announced when you open the app. New premium theme: Mint.

  • 30 September 2026 · Caffeine Break Buddy 1.0.21

    Your estimate follows what you log

    The progress estimate moves in proportion to how much caffeine you log. Reminders arrive once, at the time you picked, the cutoff reminder works, and milestone alerts arrive when a badge unlocks. New premium theme: Rooibos.

  • 28 September 2026 · The Long Night & Azor Ahai 1.1.3

    The Player Reports

    There is one Night King now: cast down by anything but a bane, he rises again in the same face and name. The September bug reports are traced and fixed.

  • 14 September 2026 · Supernatural 2.35a

    The witch you let live

    Turning down a witch hunt no longer kills the witch, and the world stops filling with new hunters and werewolves.

  • 13 September 2026 · The Long Night & Azor Ahai 1.1.2

    The Cold Does Not Fall

    The Night King no longer dies by accident, and the mod is in Spanish and French as well.

  • 11 September 2026 · The Long Night & Azor Ahai 1.1.1

    The Watch Keeps No Land

    A sworn brother renounces any land he inherits outside the Wall, the Lord Commander included.

  • 11 September 2026 · Dlonem

    The Long Night wiki goes up

    Every mechanic in the mod, with the numbers, on one page: how the invasion runs, coming back from the dead, the Night's Watch, the Last Hour, every game rule and decision.

  • 9 September 2026 · Supernatural 2.35

    The Hunt

    A kill leaves a mark on the county it happened in. One body is usually just a body; a second inside the year is not, and the county starts talking.

  • 8 September 2026 · THC Break Buddy 1.0.50

    iOS: purchases land where they should

    Premium status now syncs directly with your App Store purchase, and anyone who bought it before can restore it from Settings at no charge.

  • 4 September 2026 · Compatibility Patch 1.1.3a

    Re-checked against today's AGOT update

  • 4 September 2026 · The Long Night & Azor Ahai 1.1.0b

    Keeping pace with A Game of Thrones

    A hotfix for AGOT's 0.5.2 hotfix. The dead no longer spend a second winter proving a war they have already won.

  • 3 September 2026 · THC Break Buddy 1.0.49

    Your profile can move down, not just up

    If your logs look lighter the app offers to move your tier down — always your call. Finished a break by accident? Resume it. The recovery graph now shows the whole break, relapses included.

  • 3 September 2026 · Tiny Dragon Hoard 1.0.40

    One notification at a time

    Notices take turns instead of stacking, and never land over a menu or a take-off. “Welcome back” only appears when you were really away, and after a Keep Flying ad the sky waits for your tap.

  • 3 September 2026 · The Long Night & Azor Ahai 1.1.0a

    One Host

    The dead stop fighting the dead. Twelve fixes, no new content.

  • 2 September 2026 · The Long Night & Azor Ahai 1.1.0

    The Last Hour

    Reaching the Night King opens a Trial-of-Seven-style melee: you and your companions against him, on foot, in the snow. Lose and he keeps you.

  • 1 September 2026 · Supernatural 2.34

    The Blood Remembers Who

    Every turning is remembered — dated, in your own words, naming the one who did it.
